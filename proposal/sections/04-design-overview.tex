\subsection{Design}
\subsubsection{System overview}
EventLens is a Polymarket-informed research system for forecasting volatility and examining event-conditioned portfolio risk. It acquires prediction-market, indexed settlement and conventional financial data, preserves the evidence with its original timing, and converts it into versioned research features. Analytical services identify historically informative wallets, estimate market and wallet-informed event probabilities, map events to supported assets, and compare baseline and augmented volatility forecasts. The same validated calculations drive portfolio scenarios, the investor dashboard and a controlled research assistant. Walk-forward evaluation determines which model versions may be used. User selections and reviewed relevance feedback shape an analysis, but cannot change wallet eligibility or fitted results.
